A small problem turned into something I’m really proud of building.

A while back, I came across a discussion in the Figma Community where people were struggling to import Draw.io files into Figma in an editable format. SVGs worked… but not really couldn’t properly edit or reuse the elements. That stuck with me.

So I decided to build a solution.

I created a community plugin called “Drawio to Figma” allowing users to export their diagrams in a smarter way and bring them into Figma as fully editable components.

Fast forward a few days… I received an email showing the plugin gaining traction — users, saves, and real adoption. Seeing people actually use something I built to solve a real problem is a different kind of satisfaction.

Currently i have arrund 700+ active users and 200 to 300 views around the figma community within just few weeks of span after i released by plugin 

This experience reminded me of a few things:

- Pay attention to problems people talk about
- Build solutions, even if they seem small
- Share them — you never know who it might help

Grateful for everyone who tried it out and supported it. 🙌

You can view the plugin on the Figma Community: [Draw.io → Figma](https://www.figma.com/community/plugin/1611229062201086448/draw-io-to-figma)
